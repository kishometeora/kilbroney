# Kilbroney Forest walking map

An interactive walking map of Kilbroney Forest and Kilbroney Park, Rostrevor, built from years of our own walks in the forest.

It includes the official trails, but also forest roads, lesser-known paths and rough trails.

**Open the map:** [https://kishometeora.github.io/kilbroney/](https://kishometeora.github.io/kilbroney/)

## Features

- **Paths by type:** walkable paths, roads shared with cars, rough trails for dry weather only, and unexplored paths still to be checked
- **Route planning:** tap your way around the map and routes follow the paths, with distance, climb, walking time and a difficulty rating
- **Steepness:** routes and elevation profiles are coloured green, amber and red by gradient
- **Live location:** see where you are, and follow your progress along a planned route
- **Works offline:** once opened, the map is saved on your phone, so it works in the forest without signal
- **Wet weather mode:** hides rough trails and unexplored paths, and keeps routes off them
- **Sunset check:** shows when you'd be back, and when the hills would put your route in shadow
- **Contours and terrain:** 10 m contours and hill shading
- **Place names and points of interest:** car parks, toilets, viewpoints, picnic sites, hills and streams
- **Your own notes and recordings:** saved only on your own device
- **Light and dark modes**

## Using it on a phone

1. Open the link above in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. Add it to your home screen: on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
3. Open it once with signal so it can save itself for offline use.
4. Tap the location button and allow location access when asked.

## Please note

- This is an unofficial map, made for our own use. It isn't produced or checked by the landowners or park operators.
- Paths change. Parts of the forest have been felled in recent years, so trails can be blocked, muddy or rerouted.
- Use your own judgement on the ground, follow any signs and closures, and check the weather before you go.

## Privacy

The map doesn't collect or send any data. Saved routes, notes and recorded walks stay on your own device unless you export them.
